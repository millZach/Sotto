pianissimo  ·  as quiet as software gets

What leaves this computer

Nothing, by default. Sotto has no analytics, no crash upload, no update telemetry, and no account. Here is the complete list — including the two honest exceptions, both opt-in.

Your dataLeaves?Details
AudioNever Held in memory only and discarded the moment transcription finishes. Never written to disk.
TranscriptsNever* *Unless you turn on optional AI cleanup with your own OpenRouter key — off by default. Text only, never audio; offline, you get the raw local transcript.
HistoryNever Optional, stored locally, searchable, bounded, and clearable in one click.
Usage & telemetryNever None exists to send. No analytics, no crash reports, no phoning home.
Model downloadsOpt-in The Standard model ships in the installer — nothing downloads on first run. The optional Multi-lingual model downloads from Hugging Face after you review its size and license; that request carries ordinary metadata like your IP, nothing more.

tutti  ·  everything included

Small app, complete instrument

About 500 MB installed, with a ready-to-use speech model in the box. No Python, no setup, no model download on first run.

Two modes, both local

Standard ships in the installer — instant English dictation. Add the optional Multi-lingual mode to dictate in other languages; it downloads only with your consent.

Streaming transcription

Sotto transcribes while you talk, so even long dictations finish almost immediately after you stop.

Personal dictionary

Teach Sotto the names, products, and jargon it should always get right.

Clipboard first, always

Every transcript is copied before pasting. A blocked paste never loses a word.

Local history

Optional and searchable, with retention you control and a clear-all that means it.

Quiet by design

Lives in the menu bar or tray, follows your light or dark theme, respects reduced motion, and can launch at login.
