How the iPhone app reconnects

The question: if the app keeps the open thread on screen while it reconnects, and leaves a computer it couldn't reach alone until you press Try again, does it behave the way you expect with Forge reachable and the laptop in your backpack?

The left phone behaves the way the app does today, the right one the way it would after the change. Every button drives both, so you can compare them.

The proposed rules
  • A thread you're reading stays on screen while its computer reconnects. A quiet line says "Reconnecting to Forge…", and the fresh copy replaces it when it arrives. "Reading this thread…" shows only the first time you open a thread.
  • Once a computer fails to connect, or drops, nothing retries it on its own. Coming back to the app and pulling to refresh both skip it. Only its Try again or Reconnect button tries again. Quitting and reopening the app tries every computer once.
  • A connect gives up after 10 seconds (30 today) and says in plain words that the computer couldn't be reached.
