Direction 2 of the ten was one plate in one place that changes role: an arrow sends, a square stops, a list-plus queues. Below it as drawn, four tightened takes, and one live take with motion. What every take keeps from 9b: Enter sends, or queues while a turn runs; ⌘Enter steers; ⌘⇧S stops; and Stop keeps a button with its trace for the whole turn. 2 as drawn hid Stop once you typed during a turn; no take here does.
