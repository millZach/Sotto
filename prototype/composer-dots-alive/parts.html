Dots that listen

All ten are 10.2: the provider's mark and the model's name, one to four effort dots in the provider's colour, the shield and Send, in one pill. Each frame shows the end of a thread above the pill, so you can see the dots answer the prompt (A to E, J) or the chat (F to I).

The rules they keep: movement stays inside the dots, except G, which moves them once per turn. Nothing loops at rest. Effort changes only when you press something: B is your own words, C and J only suggest. Reduce motion turns every one of them into a plain change of state.
