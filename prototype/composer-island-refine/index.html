The island, refined

6 as drawn is a floating one-line island with a disc, which opens to the options and the branch line while pointed at or focused. Below it, four tightened takes and a live one you can type in. Each frame is the Threads composer at its real 760px width, in five states.

What every take changes from 6:

  • It opens on focus, not on hover. A pointer passing over the island no longer moves anything. Clicking it, Tab into it, typing anywhere in the transcript, a paste, a drop or dictation opens it (the same list ADR-0125 already uses). It closes when focus leaves with nothing typed and nothing queued.
  • It grows upward. The island's foot stays where it stands, so the disc becomes Send in the same corner, and the transcript keeps the full composer's room the whole time (ADR-0125 decision 3).
  • Closed, the disc says what Enter would do: a dim arrow at rest, and Stop with its trace while a turn runs, so a turn can be stopped without opening anything. Open, the keys come back: Send ↵, Steer ⌘↵, Stop ⇧⌘S, Queue ↵, as ADR-0153 has them.
  • A draft or a queued prompt keeps it open, so nothing you wrote is ever folded away.

The keyboard loses nothing: every option is one Tab from the prompt, because the prompt opening is what shows them. Escape in an empty prompt closes it.
