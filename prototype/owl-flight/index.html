The still scene costs 2.2 to 2.7% of the renderer here. Every flight holds the app's 30 frames a second. At 60 the low swoop costs 9.7%, so the candidates stay at 30. These numbers come from headless Chromium on this page, measured on the home to other tree crossing, not from the app.

My pick: C, Low swoop. It is the most owl-like: it drops off the branch, glides low and level, and brakes by climbing onto the perch. Its glide keeps one drawing for whole stretches, so it changes the fewest glyphs of all.
