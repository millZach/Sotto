The prompt box: twenty quiet directions

Every composer here holds six things: what you write, attach, the model's name, effort drawn rather than written, a shield for permissions and one round Send. The shield is filled in the warning colour while the thread has full access, and an outline when it asks first. Fast mode and Watch go into the model menu and the pane header. The branch line, key caps, chevrons and the queue tray are gone; a queued prompt is a count on Send.

The twenty differ in how effort is drawn, the box's shape, and what the effort mark does while a turn runs, so the one mark says both how hard and that it is working. Each frame is live. Click the effort mark or scroll over it to change it, click the shield, type and press Enter; Play runs it for you. Key names live in each control's tooltip and accessible name.
