The pill's actions

The pill is 10.2 with C as drawn: the provider's mark and the model, effort dots whose suggestion pulses as rings with a note, the shield, and the actions. Five directions for Send, Stop, Queue and Steer. All keep the keys (↵ sends or queues, ⌘↵ steers, ⇧⌘S stops). All keep Stop reachable for the whole turn, keep the light on whatever stops, and never move the button under the pointer as the state changes.

Each Play sends a prompt, lets the turn run, types a follow-up and holds ⌘ to show Steer, queues it, types another and steers it, then stops the turn. Hold ⌘ yourself on any frame.
