# A thread makes 3D models with Meshy

Zach asked on October 22, 2026 for something like the Meshy plugin Nous Research shows for Hermes Agent, and chose the same day: an Owl feature; a tool for every thread with a key in Settings; 3D models shown inline, on a page of their own, in Tools and on the iPhone; inline variant A (a quiet line that opens a turnable view) and page variant C (one large view and a filmstrip) of `docs/prototypes/meshy-models-prototype.html`; Tools gets its own surface; each 3D model asks first, with a Settings switch that turns asking off; Meshy's training terms are acceptable if said plainly; no stop button and no "See it in your room" yet. Answering the review, he added: threads on a paired host get the tool with a Meshy key entered on that host (ADR-0025 unchanged); a monthly credit limit of 1,000 by default; **3D models** as the name of the page, the Tools surface and the Settings section; and USDZ always made, for the iPhone. [ADR-0141](../adr/0141-a-thread-makes-3d-models-with-meshy-and-asks-before-each.md) governs behaviour; [the research note](../research/2026-10-22-meshy-api.md) has Meshy's facts.

The mock-up says "Models"; the built surfaces say **3D models**, because "model" already means the AI model a thread runs on, as Zach chose.

## Tickets, in dependency order

1. **Hold the feature, the key and the settings.** `meshy-3d-models` in `src/shared/owlFeatures.ts`. A `meshy` slot in the credential store, beside `formatting` and run.cloud's. Settings > 3D models: the key with Save, Replace and Remove, **Ask before each 3D model** (on), the monthly credit limit (1,000), this month's credits, recent 3D models, and the lines on what leaves the computer, including Meshy's training terms. The settings go in `src/shared/settings.ts` and on the patch allow-list in `registerIpc.ts`. Stable shows none of it and keeps the key. Follows `CloudIphoneSettings.tsx`.
2. **Speak Meshy's API behind one adapter.** `src/main/tools/meshy/`: create, poll and download for text-to-3D (preview, then refine) and image-to-3D, over `fetch`, pacing on `Retry-After`. A fake Meshy API under `tests/fixtures/` for every test. Downloads only from `assets.meshy.ai`, size-capped, written only inside the target folder. A record table for 3D models that follows Keep local history and forgetting a thread, keeps Meshy's task IDs inside the adapter, and picks up unfinished tasks on start for three days. Follows `src/main/tools/cloudIphone/`.
3. **Give threads `make_3d_model`, and ask first.** The tool on the thread tool server for every provider that takes Sotto's tools, on this computer's threads (a host's follow in ticket 6). The request card (words or photo, the training line, credits and the month's credits so far, **Make the model** and **Deny**), the limit, the photo re-checked and redrawn without location and camera details, and the tool's answer with the paths. With asking off, the card is replaced by a line saying what it cost. Follows `CloudIphoneRequest.tsx` and `visualTools.ts`.
4. **Draw a 3D model in the thread.** Variant A: the quiet line with the still picture, name, credits and file count, which opens the turnable view in place, with the states Asks first, Shape, Texture, Done and Failed. The view draws a GLB with WebGL from bytes main reads through a preload bridge, refusing a GLB that points outside itself. Choose the drawing code here (three.js is the likely pick, as a bundled devDependency with its notice) and say why in the PR. Keyboard: arrow keys turn, Home faces the front, Escape closes.
5. **The 3D models page and its Tools surface.** Variant C: one large view with the details, **Go to thread** and **Show in File Explorer** beside it, and a filmstrip of every 3D model below, reached from the sidebar foot. Tools > 3D models between iPhone and Terminal, for the open thread, with a dot while one is being made. Both say when a record's files are gone. `CONTEXT.md`'s Tools panel entry goes to eight surfaces.
6. **Make 3D models on a paired host.** The host saves a Meshy key in its encrypted credential file, entered on that machine the way its OpenRouter key is, and its threads get the tool with their own count and limit, always asking. The desktop draws a host thread's 3D models from bytes read over the connection Files uses, and the host's tile in Settings → Hosts says whether it has a key. The desktop never sends one.
7. **Show 3D models on the iPhone.** The record and still picture with the thread; the USDZ in the app's own 3D view when pressed, carried over the phone connection only then and cached in the app. Mind host protocol version 1: an older phone ignores the new item and a newer phone reads its absence. No AR.
8. **Write it down.** `docs/guide.md` gains a 3D models section; a verification note in `docs/verification/` with its screenshots in `artifacts/meshy-3d-models/`; `README.md`'s "What it does" gains the feature once it ships in Owl.

1 and 2 run side by side; 3 follows both; 4, 6 and 7 follow 3; 5 follows 4; 8 closes.

## Acceptance checks for the whole

- On the Windows laptop with a Meshy key on a paid plan, an Owl build asks before a 3D model from words and one from a photo, makes both into `models/`, shows them inline, on the page, in Tools and on the iPhone, counts their credits, and refuses one past the limit before sending anything.
- With asking off, a thread makes one without asking and the thread says what it cost.
- On forge, with a Meshy key entered there, a thread asks, makes a 3D model into its folder on forge, and the desktop and the iPhone show it.
- Quitting while a 3D model is being made and starting again finishes it.
- A stable build shows nothing of it and keeps the key and the records.
- No log or diagnostic record holds the key, the words or the photo, and no record holds the key or the photo. The words stay only in the 3D model's own record, which follows Keep local history. The only new hosts in a network capture are `api.meshy.ai` and `assets.meshy.ai`.
- Light, dark and reduced motion at 1600x1000, 1280x800 and 820x560 for the line, the view, the page, the Tools surface and Settings; the keyboard path through each.
- Each ticket runs the four CI gates and the Playwright specs it touches, gets a two-axis review, and leaves a verification note where it changed what the running app shows.
