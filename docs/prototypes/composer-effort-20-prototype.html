Effort and speed without dots: twenty directions

Every frame is the quiet pill at real size, and every frame shares one state, so changing effort in one changes all of them. Hover any control to hear what it says, Tab to it and use ← → for effort, ⌥+↑ ↓ for speed, Enter to open a menu and Esc to close it. Pressing the model name steps to the next model, so 3, 5, 6 and 8 levels are one press apart. The strip under each frame shows the same control at Standard, Fast and Ultra fast. The bar at the bottom always says which direction you are on and what hover, focus and a press do there. In one-at-a-time view, [ and ] step through.

Why move off the dots: today, six references, and what was cut

Today, on Drake's screen at 11:25 pm (Owl, Watcher's composer)

Eight 5.4px dots on a 9px pitch, in a second row under the model name (EffortDots.tsx, quietComposer.css). At Fast they turn purple; at Ultra fast a brighter purple with a glow.

  • Hierarchy. Everything in the pill sits on one line except this block, which stacks two rows into Send's 32px axis. The row of dots reads as a caption to the model, so the quietest thing on the pill is also the busiest.
  • Density. Five to eight identical marks. To read "which level", you count. Lit and unlit differ only by opacity (100% against 22%).
  • Affordance. Nothing says the dots can be pressed. Changing speed is hidden twice: you press the dot that is already lit, or you know ⌥+↑.
  • Accessibility. Each dot's target is 9 by 10 pixels, under WCAG 2.2's 24 by 24 minimum. Unlit dots at 22% fall under 3:1 for graphics. Only the tooltip says the level in words.
  • Speed is colour alone. Provider colour, then purple, then brighter purple. A colour-blind user can't see the change, and Grok's own colour is lavender, close to Fast's purple.
  • Placement. The dots line up with the name rather than the mark, a 21.2px inset that reads as misaligned at a glance. Eight levels make the dot row wider than a short model name.
  • What today gets right, and every direction has to match: no words, one press to any level, it stays out of the prompt's way at four levels or fewer, and it never moves.

What was cut, in every direction below

  • Purple for speed, and its glow. Speed reads without colour: words, a bolt, a lean, a hatch, chevrons or a knob's shape. Only 12 uses colour, and it uses the theme's accent rather than purple.
  • The row under the name. Everything stays on the pill's one line, or moves to the top edge, the header or the placeholder. Nothing sits underneath.
  • Counting. Where a word, a height, an arc or a fill can show the amount, it does.
  • Provider colour on the effort mark. The provider's colour stays on its mark. Effort is drawn in the theme's text ink, so it reads on any theme.
  • Borders, chevrons and glow. The chip in 11 is filled rather than outlined (ADR-0110). The model keeps no chevron (ADR-0161).
  • Still open: ADR-0161's suggestion rings ("Suggests High") need a new home in any direction picked, either the next step drawn faintly or a line in the tooltip.
