The prompt box: twenty minimal, live directions

The island showed that hiding the options until you click only postpones them: closed it was a line, open it was today's composer. So each of these twenty removes or merges something instead, and its motion says something: a sent prompt leaving, a turn starting, a follow-up joining the queue. Only 8 hides anything, and it says so.

Every frame is live. Click and type, or press Play to watch a scripted turn: a prompt is typed and sent, the turn runs, a follow-up is typed and queued, and it sends when the turn ends. Enter sends or queues, ⌘Enter steers, Tab takes the next prompt, ↑↓ in an empty prompt turn through the suggestions, Esc leaves. Clicking the model or the effort cycles it. Hold ⌘ for 3, 8 and 20.

Common to all twenty: the branch line under the card is gone. The pane header already names the folder, so the branch joins it there. Every frame shows that header.
