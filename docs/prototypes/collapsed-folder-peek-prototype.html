Muse

Three ways to peek are in the switcher below. The folders on the left are closed, as they start. Rest on Sotto for a moment: the peek should tell you which thread is working and which one is waiting on you, without opening the folder. Pressing a thread in the peek opens it here.

Try it

  1. Rest on Sotto until the peek lands. Sweep straight down the folders: nothing should flash open.
  2. Move diagonally from the folder to a thread in the peek and press it. The folder stays closed - check the State strip.
  3. Hover Workshop (12 threads): where does the list end, and is that ending honest?
  4. Turn on Short window and hover the lowest folder. Then Archive lab (empty) and Field notes (one, done).
  5. Keyboard only: Tab to a folder, wait, ArrowDown into the peek, Enter to open, Esc to put a peek away.
  6. Click a folder to open it for real, then hover it again: no peek. Type login in Search: search still opens folders; the peek stands down.
  7. With a peek open on Sotto, press Finish a working thread above: the row and the folder's dots change under your pointer.
