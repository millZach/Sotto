iPhone Duo layouts

Captured on the iPhone Duo simulator (iOS 27.1) with the UI fixture. Unfolded is landscape, 951×669 pt, size class regular, with 867 pt left for the app beside the system's tab rail. Folded is 382×644 pt, compact, and stays exactly as it is today.

Today

1. List and thread (recommended)

Threads on the left at 360 pt, the open thread on the right. The tab rail stays.

2. Two threads

One thread on each half of the fold. The right page reads "Reading this thread…" because the app holds one open thread's conversation at a time; this one needs a model change.

3. Watcher beside

Watcher becomes a standing page on the right. The left keeps the phone's own Threads stack. No Watcher tab.

4. Reading measure

The phone layout kept, every page held to 620 pt in the middle. The smallest change.

5. Needs you column

The open thread, and on the right every question and permission waiting anywhere. The Threads home sits behind the list button.

6. Sidebar

The tab bar becomes a labelled sidebar, with the threads at work listed under it.

7. Board

The Threads home as three columns: Needs you, Working now, Recent. A thread opens full width at 680 pt.

8. Thread strip

One thread at a reading measure, with every live thread as a mark down a thin strip on the left. The top mark goes back to the Threads home.
