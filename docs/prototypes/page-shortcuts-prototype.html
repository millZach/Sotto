Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+5 (⌘1 to ⌘5 on a Mac) open Dictate, Watcher, Threads, Usage and Settings while a Sotto window is in front. Keyboard

Click a shortcut and press the new keys; let go to save. These keys work while Sotto is in front, and stand down in terminals and dialogs. Dictation’s keys always win a tie.

How it behaves

  • Same machinery as thread shortcuts. Same recorder, same rules (needs Ctrl, or ⌘/⌃ on a Mac), same saved setting. A row left out follows its default; a removed row has no shortcut, and the page links still work.
  • Gated pages leave their rows. Watcher and Usage are Owl features, so stable shows neither row and their keys do nothing. Dictate’s row goes while dictation is turned off. Flip Stable above to see it.
  • Works on every page, in a text box too (as the thread shortcuts do), but not inside a terminal, an open dialog, or the browser pane, which keep their keys.
  • A press opens the page the way its link does. For Dictate, Watcher and Threads, focus lands on that page’s lit room tab, as after pressing the room switch.
  • Nothing else uses Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+5 today. No menu, terminal or browser-pane key; dictation’s keys would still win a tie.
