Duo tabs · prototype, round 4

A thread on the unfolded Duo gets a sidebar of threads, as on the desktop. Sideways, it stands beside the thread, and the button at the thread bar's left collapses it for full-width reading. Upright, the thread has the screen; swipe in from the left edge (or press the edge handle) to bring the sidebar over it. The open thread sits on the same soft chip as the chosen tab. Press any thread in a sidebar to switch to it.

Tabs are your M pick. The sideways rail is on the right, as you chose in round 2; Left at the top moves it, as the Settings row will. With the sidebar open the thread is about 590 points wide, under its 680-point reading width; collapsed it takes the reading width again. Regular iPhones don't change.

Round 4: New thread’s + leaves the Threads page for the rail, in the strip under the clock and battery, set apart from the tabs. Unfolded sideways it tops the rail the same way; upright it leads the row along the bottom. Pick where it shows and how it looks.

Round 2, your pick A: icons only, the chosen one filled on a soft rounded square, no pill. Folded, the rail stands centred down the right strip. Unfolded sideways, it stands in the same strip under the clock, on the right unless Settings says Left. Unfolded upright, the tabs run along the bottom. Pick the icon size.

Colours are the Glass theme's. Every tab keeps a 44-point press area at least. Press any tab in any screen to choose it there, and Right or Left in the sideways Settings screen to move that rail. Watcher's composer shows the fade under it from #1868, the fix for the stray “at”. A long press on a tab shows its name large, and VoiceOver reads it.
