The built-in palettes’ text, before and after

Every text pair the window paints now reads at 4.5:1 or better in all twelve halves of Sotto, Hush, Linen, Nocturne, Tropic and Citrine. This changes how Windows and macOS look, on purpose; the design baselines need refreshing on Windows.

Most of it is the dark halves’ muted text, lifted a little, and their bubbles, lowered a step. In light mode the amber, green and red text darkens, and a hovered link darkens in four palettes. Picked lines in Changes keep their selection wash; their numbers and signs take more ink instead. Hue and chroma never move: only lightness, and only where a pair fell short.
