Can the two PICs on segment 3 come out without dropping the trunk? Grok said no.

Claude Code

Yes, if they are power conditioners and not isolators. A PIC on that trunk is a pass-through: pulling one breaks the segment for the racks past it, but the trunk itself recovers once the bridge is fitted.

  • Fit the bridge before pulling the second unit, not after.
  • Do it on an outage, or accept a short glitch on the downstream PIDs.

One thing to confirm before cutting: that neither unit is an FBI isolator. Removing one of those is the case where Grok's "not allowed" applies.
