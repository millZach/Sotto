Threads as the sidebar list · prototype

On an upright iPhone and the folded Duo, the Threads tab becomes the list the unfolded Duo shows beside a thread: Needs you, Working now and Recent, a row each, in place of today’s cards. Press a row to open the thread full screen; Back returns to the list. The unfolded Duo keeps the list beside the open thread, as in #1904, shown last for comparison.

The header folds in #1849: no counts line, the computer menu in the search row, unreachable computers in one line. Settled stays folded at the end. Whichever Needs you row you pick is the row the Duo’s sidebar shows too, so there is one list everywhere. Colours are the Glass theme’s.
