New composer directions

Four directions beside 00, Drake's composer as it ships on Owl today. Each sits in a real thread pane with a long transcript behind it. Use the bar to switch light and dark, idle and working, and the pane's width. Every frame is live: type in it, open its menus, set effort to Max to see the slow top-level trace. The strip under each frame shows all four states at once. A and B are close cousins, as T3 Code and Codex are; C and D move further. Mix freely: "A's footer with D's working line" is a fine answer.

What the three reference apps do (research, with what could not be checked)

Read October 30, 2026 from each vendor's own docs, footage and source. Every frame here drops their 1px hairline: Sotto's composer has no border in any state.

T3 Code (source of pingdotgg/t3code at 80a2351, and the repo's marketing screenshot)

  • Two rows in one rounded card (rounded-2xl, max 46rem, frosted fill): the prompt, then a footer. Paperclip left; the provider mark, model and effort as plain words right ("Opus 5.5 High"); then a round Send.
  • Permissions are hidden while they match your default. Otherwise an icon and label in the footer. The effort menu also holds a Permissions section.
  • Send is a filled circle with an up arrow, grey when empty. While running and empty it is a red circle with a square (Interrupt). Once you type it becomes Steer or Queue, and holding Ctrl or Cmd flips it.
  • Queued messages show at the end of the transcript as dashed, muted bubbles, not in the composer. The working line ("Waiting on 4 subagents… Stop") sits above the composer in the transcript.
  • Where the work runs (environment, worktree, branch) is a muted text strip under the card, shown on new threads only by default.
  • Not verified: how running, steer and permission states look (no screenshot shows them; source only).

Codex (learn.chatgpt.com docs and their illustrations, OpenAI video posters)

  • Two rows: the prompt (about two lines at rest), then one row inside the box: + and the approval mode on the left ("Auto-review", "Full access" in orange), the model and effort as words on the right ("6 Sol Medium", effort dimmer, ⚡ for Fast), a mic, then a filled round Send.
  • The model and effort open a small panel: a six-stop slider from Faster to Smarter, "Advanced" for a specific model and effort, and a ⚡ speed toggle.
  • Stop takes Send's place: a white disc with a dark square. Queued messages sit above the composer, where you can edit, reorder, send or delete them; steer or queue by default is a setting. Goals and subagents attach as rows on top of the box.
  • Box: about 24px radius, filled, transcript-width at the pane's foot.
  • Not verified: what a queued message looks like; openai.com and chatgpt.com refused automated reads. The docs say the worktree control is under the composer while the newest illustration draws it above; the illustrations are OpenAI's HTML recreations, not raw captures.

Claude Code desktop (code.claude.com docs, Anthropic's weekly what's-new footage to September 2026)

  • A one-line field, and the settings outside it in a row under the box: + and the mic on the left with the permission mode as a word ("Auto"); the model and effort as two separate words on the right ("Opus 5 High"); a small plan-usage ring.
  • Send is a small grey return glyph inside the field's right edge, not a disc. Stop is an outlined square in the same place.
  • Working state lives in the transcript: the orange spark and a verb ("Writing summary…", "Migrating… · 1s").
  • Cmd+Shift+M, I and E open the mode, model and effort menus.
  • Not verified: the current layout that puts the environment first in that row (docs text only, no screenshot); which rule chooses return glyph or square while working; how a steering message reads.
