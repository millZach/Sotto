Prototype pages on a question

Sotto finds the prototype pages in a question and in the agent's message before it: an .html file in the thread's folder, a file:// link, or a localhost address. Each one opens in your browser on the computer, and on the iPhone through phone access. The agent doesn't have to do anything. Pick how the page should sit on the question. The state buttons show what happens when something is wrong.
