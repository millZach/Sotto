What it covers here

    Placement trade-offs

    P1 · Top centre, under the bar

    Hangs from the bar's centre, under Sotto's indicator, where the shell already opens the clock's popup. It only ever covers the tops of the windows that meet in the middle: a header, a title, a terminal's oldest lines. It moves only when you drag it, needs nothing from Hyprland but the focused monitor, and keeps clear of notifications (top right) and the OSD (bottom centre). It is the furthest from the prompt you are looking at.

    P2 · Bottom centre of the focused window Dropped

    It sits on the focused window's input, which is where your words go: Sotto's composer, Claude Code's prompt. It hides the text you are adding to, in every layout. It also has to follow focus, which P5’s card costs out.

    P3 · Bottom right of the screen, lifted

    Near where your eyes are, and never on the seam. Lifted 150 px it clears a composer at its resting height, but it lands on the right end of the latest reply, and a composer that grows with a long draft grows into it. Dropped into the corner itself, it would cover Sotto's send and stop button whenever Sotto is on the right.

    P4 · Top right, under the bar's right group

    Covers little in the windows. But it is exactly where notifications appear (top right, 380 px wide, the same anchor) and where the network, Bluetooth and audio panels open. A notification while you dictate lands on the pill or under it. Tick “A notification arrives” to see it.

    P5 · Top centre of the focused window Added

    P2's idea without P2's problem: the pill rides on the window your words go to, over its title instead of its input. The cost is movement and plumbing. It jumps whenever focus moves, so the plugin follows Hyprland's focus and move events, and Hyprland's event socket has no resize event, so a split resized by hand leaves it behind until the next one. When the focused window is at the top right, it lands where notifications appear.

    P0 · Before: bottom centre of the screen

    Where #839 drew it. In a split it straddles the seam and covers both windows' bottom inner corners: Sotto's send and stop button when Sotto is on the left, the terminal's prompt when the terminal is. Kept here only to compare.

    How the plugin would anchor it

    

    
    What the desktop shows
    
    
      
      
    • Omarchy's 26 px bar and Hyprland's tiling: 10 px outer and 5 px inner gaps, 2 px borders, the focused window's border in the theme's accent. Three windows are a master on the left and a stack on the right, with Sotto at the bottom of the stack.
      • 
      
    • The terminal runs Claude Code and is scrolled to its latest lines, so its prompt sits at the bottom, as it does once a session is under way. Sotto's transcript and composer use the real 760 px column.
      • 
      
    • Red marks something you need while dictating: where your words go, Sotto's send and stop button, the bar's indicators, a notification. Amber marks something worth seeing: the reply you are answering, another window's input. Anything else under the pill is listed, not marked.
      • 
      
    • The failed state is the widest pill and the one that waits for you, so it is here as well as listening and transcribing. Its second button says Discard, because that is what a press does to a kept recording.
      • 
      
    • If the bar is moved to the bottom (omarchy bar position), P1 would follow it to the bottom centre, back on the seam. The plugin should then keep to the top edge.

    Decided on #850
    
    
    
      
    P1 is where the pill starts. Top centre, just under the bar, hanging from Sotto's indicator.
    
      
    It moves like the Windows pill. Drag it, and on release it snaps to the nearest screen edge and keeps its place along that edge, as snapToEdge does on Windows. Sotto saves the place, so the pill comes back where you left it. A layer-shell surface can change its margins while it shows, so the plugin can do this. This page does not draw the drag.
    
      
    It follows the focused display. The pill shows on Hyprland's focused monitor. Omarchy's focus follows the mouse, so the pill moves to the display the mouse is on, even while you dictate, and a saved edge place applies on whichever display shows it. The bar indicator shows on every display's bar.
    
    
    

    
    Designer's recommendation
    
    
    
      
    P1, top centre under the bar. It is the only placement that covers nothing you need in every layout and every focus here, with or without a notification on screen. It stays put until you drag it, and it reads as part of the bar it hangs from, beside the indicator that carries the same state, in the place the shell already uses for the centre group's popups. Its cost is distance: it is far from a prompt at the bottom of the screen. The dictation hotkey, not a trip with the mouse, is how most dictations end.
    
      
    P5 is the runner-up if the pill should show which window your words go to. It earns that with a focus listener and a pill that jumps.
