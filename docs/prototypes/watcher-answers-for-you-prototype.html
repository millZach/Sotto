PROTOTYPE

Watcher answers for you

Sotto issue #2237 · throwaway

Plan: a Settings › Watcher switch, off by default, lets Watcher answer a watched thread’s question card when your rules or the thread’s work already say what to pick. Pick how you see each answer and put it right: A, B or C.

Show

Assumptions and what each variant trades
  • A · A receipt in the conversation. Each answer sits with the reply that gave it, so it reads in order with everything else Watcher did. It scrolls away with the conversation; there is no one place to look later.
  • B · A list you review. The room stays quietest: one line in the conversation, one entry on the strip. Answers wait in one list until you mark them seen. If you never open it, you never see them.
  • C · A short hold first. The only one with a real undo: for two minutes you can stop the answer or give your own, and nothing is sent. Every answer costs two minutes, and an answer you only notice later still needs a correction.
  • In all three, an answer already sent can’t be taken back. Change answer sends the thread “Zach changed the answer: …” in your name and tells Watcher. The thread’s pane shows that message as sent by Sotto for you.
  • Watcher leaves you questions about how something looks or how Sotto behaves, and every permission request. Tray tint and Docs sweep show that.
  • The rules, files, threads and times are made up for the demo. Only Sotto’s default theme is drawn. The night scene is left out.
