PROTOTYPE · iPhone Duo polish, Watcher without its bar

Unfolded Duo, upright and sideways

Both screens are live. The app reaches every edge: the page's ground runs up under the clock, with no band. The rail stands on the right: Sidebar and New thread at the top, the four tabs low in one pill where your right thumb rests, every icon one size in the same 50-point tile.

Upright, drag the sidebar with your thumb: drag right anywhere on a thread, Watcher, Computers or Settings to pull the threads in; it follows your thumb. Drag left, or press beside it, to push it away. The rail's top button does the same. On the Threads page the list is already the page, so that button rests. Sideways, the sidebar stands beside the thread and the same button hides it. Press the model pill in any reply box for its sheet.

Watcher has no bar

Watcher opens on its conversation, full screen, like any thread. The Conversation | Watched bar and the Watched view are gone. Here is where each thing in them went:

  • The owl's "go to the top": press the Watcher tab again, as every other tab already does.
  • The owl's light (working or not): on the Watcher tab's owl, and on the state pill beside Watcher's name at the top of the conversation.
  • Watched threads: in the Threads list, where each one already carries the small owl after its provider.
  • Stop watching one thread, or Watch a thread: that thread's own Watch button, as today.
  • Stop all (stop the threads Watcher started): a row in the sheet the reply box's model pill opens, above New Watcher. It asks first, as it does now.
  • New Watcher stays the sheet's last row. Choose Watcher's computer stays on the line under Watcher's name.
  • The "3 of 4 watched" meter and the "No longer watched" list go with the Watched view.

Same on every iPhone: the folded Duo and a plain iPhone below have no bar either.

Rail icons: before and after

Before, the owl filled its whole 28-point box, while the system's symbols keep a margin, so the owl read a size larger. After, every tab's icon is drawn to one optical size, about 20 points of ink in a 24-point frame, in a 50-point tile, all four in one pill.

Reply box: room above the model pill

Dashed blue is where a press types; dashed orange is where a press opens the model picker. Press near the bottom of the words in each box and read what happened under it. After, there is a 12-point gap above the row, the words take every press above the row, and the pill's press area stops at the row's top. The same box is used by threads and Watcher, on every iPhone.

"Working" stays above the reply box

Before, the page followed the newest words, not its end, so Watcher's working row sat under the box with only its top showing. After, the page's end is the working row (and any follow-ups under it), and the space under the page is the box's height as it is now, so the row keeps the same gap above the box at one line, five lines, or with pictures. A thread's own working row is inside its last run, which already ends the page; the after build checks it at every box height too.
