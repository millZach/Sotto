  • Sotto's creature only shows while a thread is Monitoring, Working in the background, Waiting or Babysitting. There is no creature while a thread thinks, writes, is done or has an error, so those are the states that get a pose.
  • Reduce motion: he holds each pose still, the turn-finished flex is a still pose for a moment, and a click shows a still angry pose with no chunk.
  • Clicking him never takes focus from the composer, and nothing lies over it that catches the mouse.
  • His colours come from the theme: tan skin is the Claude provider colour, his trunks the theme accent. Babysitting mutes him, as it mutes every creature.
