How Antigravity signs in, and how its models are picked

Three answers. Each shows the same two surfaces: Antigravity’s row in Settings › Providers, and a thread’s composer with its model menu open on Antigravity’s models. Switch with the bar at the bottom, ?variant=A|B|C, or the Left and Right keys.

Nothing here reaches the network. Every sign-in, Google page and key check is fake: any code or key works, and one with “wrong” in it is refused. Gemini Enterprise’s browser step finishes with a dashed Prototype button. Antigravity’s mark and colour are placeholders drawn for this draft.
