Type and corner scale: today and three candidates (#1833)

Recommendation: A, Snap. Nine named sizes and seven corner sizes, and the app looks the same: of 1,289 size declarations, 897 stay put, 356 move half a pixel, and the 36 that move further are rare headings, figures and tiny marks. B is a cleaner ramp, but it shrinks the text you read most (the transcript, the composer, the journal and settings labels) from 15 to 14 px. C copies Windows: 13 px labels grow to 14 while 15 px reading text shrinks to 14, so the hierarchy flattens, and cards lose their soft corner (14 to 8 px). Sotto's quiet density matters more than matching Fluent. Name the tokens --tt-type-*; --tt-text-* already names text colours.

Each surface below is drawn once, with every size and corner read from today's stylesheets, then mapped onto the chosen scale. The real Windows capture sits beside it so you can see the mock matches the app. Colour is the default theme's, copied in as values.

dictate.css alone sets twelve sizes: 11, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 22 and 28 px. At 1600 the stats stand in a 300 px column with the chart, streak and where; under 900 px they sit above the journal; under 600 px the figures drop to 22 px.

2. Settings: a card with rows, descriptions and a switch

The room title is 28, the intro 16, the group heading 14, each row's label 15 and its description 13; the field is 14 and the button 13.5. The card's corner is --tt-radius-lg (14), the field and button 6, the switch 5 with a 3 px thumb.

3. Threads: the sidebar, a pane header, the transcript and the composer

The sidebar sets 12, 12.5, 13.5, 14 and 14.5; its rows round at 7 and 8. The pane title is 15 over a 12.5 path; the transcript reads at 15; the request card is 15 and 14 over 13 px code; the composer is 15 with a 13 model chip and an 11.5 key. The composer has no edge in any state.

4. A dialog: New thread's project chooser

Three levels of corner in one view: the dialog at 20, its choices at 8 and the keys at 4, with a 2 px focus line under the search. Its text is 13, 11.5 and 11 px: the smallest in the Threads room.
