Every state

The same box, drawn at each screen's width. The + , mic and Send or Stop never leave their places.

The motion, frame by frame

0.2 seconds, the same as the desktop's slim composer. With Reduce Motion it changes at once. The foot and the row's buttons stay put: only the top edge comes down, the model pill fades, and the words slide into the row.

What stays still

  • The conversation does not move when the box shrinks or grows. The box keeps its full room under the conversation, so at the very end of a thread there is about 38 points of open room above the small box.
  • The readout above the buttons measures this live: moved 0 pt by the box after each change.
  • While it works, words keep streaming in the same place throughout.
  • Queued messages, Watch notices and computer banners keep their places above the box's full room.
  • A permission change waiting on the agent, a photo, a file, typed words or the mic keeps the box full.
  • Glass on iOS 26 uses the system's own surface, which this page can't draw. It gets the same shape and is checked in the real captures.
