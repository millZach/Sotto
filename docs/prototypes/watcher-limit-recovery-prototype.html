Three variants of where Watcher's room says its provider is limited or down, and how it comes back. Each shows four states. Issue #2234. Switch variants with the bar at the bottom or ← →.

Assumptions
  1. Today is Thursday, November 5, 2:41 PM. Watcher hit Claude Code's weekly limit at 9:12 AM; it resets Monday at 3:00 PM. Titles, counts and news are made up.
  2. The turn that first meets the limit or the failure posts no reply. Its stop becomes the one notice. Only B adds a line to the conversation, once.
  3. Held news goes to Watcher in one message when it's back: one turn, not one per news.
  4. A message you send while Watcher rests waits, and goes at the reset ahead of the held news.
  5. While failing, Sotto's tries are a small check, not a Watcher turn. The raw error shows only under Details.
  6. Watcher itself is always restarted first. "By itself" or "asks first" is about the watched threads only.
  7. A thread waiting on you is never restarted. It stays in the needs-you count.
  8. In state 3, A and C look alike on purpose: once nothing needs doing, they go quiet. B also says "Back" in the strip.
  9. News shows as one muted line each in the conversation. Colours: the screenshot palette by night, Clay by day.
