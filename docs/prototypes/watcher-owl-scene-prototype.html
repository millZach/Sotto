Watcher is a thread that starts and briefs your other threads, and keeps an eye on them while they work.

It runs on your own Codex, Claude Code or Grok Build account, with that provider’s own tools. It hands changes to your threads rather than making them itself, and only you answer a thread’s questions and permissions.

Pick its model and what it may do without asking below, then send what you want done first. That message starts it.
