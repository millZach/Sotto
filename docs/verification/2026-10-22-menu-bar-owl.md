# The menu bar owl, October 22, 2026

The macOS menu bar icon of a Sotto Owl build became the menu bar owl ([ADR-0112](../adr/0112-the-menu-bar-owl-is-alive.md), from variant A of `docs/prototypes/menubar-owl-prototype.html`): the Sotto mark's owl, in colour, on a wooden branch, drawn at 1x and shown pixel-doubled. Everything below was measured on one Mac (macOS 27, a notched 16-inch display whose bar window is 33 points tall, system menu bar spacing tightened to 2), in a throwaway AppKit status item, in a bare Electron 43.8.3 app running the app's own frames and animator, and in the built app for the Settings choices. The owl in the full app's own tray was not run (see Not covered).

## What was checked

- **Placement in the status item.** macOS gives the item 1 point of padding each side, and draws the image at its own size, centred. An image up to 22 points tall stays level: at 20 and 22 points its centre stays 22.0 points below the top of the bar's window, at 24 points it moves up to 21.0 and at 26 points to 20.0, because the item grows. The frames are 22 points tall.
- **Level and spaced like Apple's own icons, with the app's own frames.** The rest frame of slate with teal eyes, of ink with amber eyes and of light with ice eyes, as `owlFramePngs` makes them from `resources/tray/owl`, sit 3.0 points from both edges of their item, where Apple's Wi-Fi, battery, Control Centre and sparkle symbols sit 2.5 to 3.0. Their centre is 22.0 points below the top of the bar's window; the symbols' are 21.75 to 22.0.
- **Width.** In Electron, a tray made from the one-tint template is 18 points wide; once the owl starts it is 29.
- **The frames.** 73 poses, each drawn for the nine body and eye colours (360 KB in all). For all 73 poses in each of the nine colours, the generator's pixels differ from the prototype's own 1x frames by 0 in every colour channel, and a run of the generator refuses to write frames that differ from them by more than 2.
- **The schedule.** Over 200 seeds of 10 simulated minutes each, a flap started 5.000 to 9.999 seconds after the one before, 7.5 seconds on average, never inside another flap; the icon changed 160.7 times a minute, and never stood still for more than 6.2 seconds.
- **Cost of the app's own animator.** Two 3-minute stretches on a bare Electron 43.8.3 tray, in ink with amber eyes: 525 and 476 swaps (175 and 159 a minute) cost the main process 0.84% and 0.78% of one core (about 2.9 ms a swap). With reduced motion on it swapped nothing in 30 seconds and used 0.03%. WindowServer's share is not counted.
- **Starting and changing colours.** Reading the manifest and one palette, making its 73 images and showing the rest frame took 29 ms. A change of colours took 26 ms the first time and 0.1 ms when that palette's frames were still kept.
- **The two choices in Settings → Appearance, in the built app.** `tests/e2e/menu-bar-owl.spec.ts`, on a Mac with the Owl feature on: Menu bar owl shows Body (Slate, Ink, Light) and Eyes (Sotto teal, Ice blue, Amber) with the saved choices pressed; a press and the Enter key save them (`menuBarOwlBody`, `menuBarOwlEyes`), and a restart keeps them. A second run opens that same profile with the feature off: nothing of it shows and the saved choices (ink, amber) stay. The screenshot is `artifacts/menu-bar-owl/settings-appearance.png`.
- **Screenshots.** The bar with the owl, dark, light and over a wallpaper: slate with teal eyes (`artifacts/menu-bar-owl/bars-slate-teal.png`) and ink with amber eyes (`artifacts/menu-bar-owl/bars-ink-amber.png`); every frame of slate with teal eyes (`artifacts/menu-bar-owl/frames-slate-teal.png`) and of ink with amber eyes (`artifacts/menu-bar-owl/frames-ink-amber.png`); the Settings choices (`artifacts/menu-bar-owl/settings-appearance.png`).
- **The plan.** Muse checked it before it was built: the first run timed out without an answer, a shorter one found seven things. The animator now keeps one state for "paused" and "reduced motion" (neither resumes while the other holds), ignores a timer callback that fires after it was cleared, never sets a zero-delay timer, stops for good rather than throw out of a timer, and writes an `sRGB` chunk in its PNGs; the glue keeps the last three palettes' finished images and the flap steps are documented as each lasting `ms`.

## Covered by unit tests

`tests/unit/main/tray/owlPoses.test.ts`: the pose table, its 73 poses and the rest pose as frame 0. `tests/unit/main/tray/owlSchedule.test.ts`: the same schedule for the same seed; flaps 5 to 10 seconds apart, start to start, and never one inside another; a blink's gap counted from the end of the blink, as in the prototype; only poses from the table; no repeated pose; a two-hour jump, as after sleep, ending at once; about 100 to 220 swaps a minute. `tests/unit/main/tray/owlFrames.test.ts`: the manifest, pixel doubling and the PNGs, and the committed palette; a file that inflates past its promised size refused while it inflates; a picture of the wrong size. `tests/unit/main/tray/owlAnimator.test.ts`: one timer at a time; a new image only when the frame changes; reduced motion showing the rest frame with no timer; pausing and resuming, and neither resuming while the other holds; a change of colours keeping the old set when the new one cannot load; a tray or a schedule that throws stopping it for good, including a tray that refuses the first picture (no start event, no timer) or the rest frame a resume shows; no zero-delay timer; dispose. `tests/unit/main/tray/menuBarOwl.test.ts` and `menuBarOwlElectron.test.ts`: the owl wanted only on a Mac in an Owl build; frames that cannot be read leaving the tray alone; reduced motion from Sotto's setting and from the system, live; the screen locking and the Mac sleeping; colour changes and the kept palettes; one image with a 2x and a 1x picture; the system or a watcher that throws, at start, on a settings change or inside a callback, stopping the owl where it is without throwing out of it; a stop that fails not blocking the others; a screen already locked at start; a palette that cannot be read tried once per change of choice. `tests/unit/release/trayOwlAssets.test.ts`: the manifest against the poses, the nine palettes, the swatches against the drawing. `tests/unit/renderer/features/settings/menuBarOwlSettings.test.tsx`: the choices hidden off a Mac and when the feature is off, and what a press saves. `tests/unit/shared/settings.test.ts`, `owlFeatures.test.ts`, `tests/unit/main/runningVersion.test.ts` and `updateService.test.ts`: the two settings and the feature.

## Not covered

- **The owl in the full app's own tray.** The wiring in `src/main/index.ts` (start after the tray, follow settings, dispose before the tray is destroyed) was typechecked and run in the built app with the tray absent, as an end-to-end run has none; the same glue and animator ran on a real tray only in the bare Electron app above. A second full Sotto beside a running one would share its data, so the check is a run of the first Owl build on a Mac: the colour owl is in the bar, 29 points wide and level with its neighbours; it flaps within 10 seconds; Settings → Appearance changes its colours at once; locking the screen stops it and unlocking starts it; Reduce motion stills it; Quit removes it with no error.
- Reduced motion switched on and off in macOS while the app runs, and the screen locking or the Mac sleeping (unit-tested against stand-ins only). Likewise a screen already locked when Sotto starts: the code asks `powerMonitor.getSystemIdleState`, and a screensaver may read as locked.
- The owl with its menu open, and at the moment a menu opens while a flap plays.
- The owl on other Macs: an external display at 1x (the 1x pictures are made by the same code and drawn in the prototype, not seen in a status item), a bar with many other items, and a bar over a busy wallpaper in a real translucent menu bar.
