# First-run setup and the owl

October 8, 2026, branch `feat/onboarding-and-logo` (ADR-0064, ADR-0063).

## The owl

The owl in `build/icon.svg` was traced from the owner's brand sheet as cubic curves on the 96-unit tile. Rendered at 800 pixels and compared pixel by pixel with the sheet's app icon, cropped to its tile, the two owls overlap by 98.4 percent; the rest is the raster's edge softening. `node scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs` then wrote every derivative. These were opened and looked at: the 1024-pixel PNG, the installer sidebar, the 16- and 32-pixel menu-bar templates, the iPhone app icon (1024 pixels, RGB with no alpha, as App Store Connect requires) and Android's square and round launcher icons at the largest and smallest densities. The Windows ICO was written by the same unchanged code from the same master and its entries were not opened one by one. At 16 pixels the full owl's beak turns grey and its pupils vanish, so the menu-bar template drops the beak and draws larger pupils, and that version reads as an owl at both sizes. The widget's twelve reference images were regenerated with `SOTTO_UPDATE_WIDGET_BASELINES=1 npx playwright test tests/e2e/visual-previews.spec.ts`, and the owl reads at the capsule's 22 pixels in light and dark.

## Setup in the running app

The built app was launched on fresh end-to-end profiles and walked from the welcome through Finish setup and all four stops of the Threads tour, with clicks, at 1280 by 800 dark, 820 by 560 dark and 1600 by 1000 light. No page errors were logged. Each step's heading took focus as it opened, the progress read "Step N of 9" with the step's name, and each forward button said what a press did: Continue on a finished step, Skip for now on the agents step, where the end-to-end fixture publishes no client status for this computer and the step says it has no word yet. Choosing Tropic on the look step repainted the window and the strip's owl at once. At 820 by 560 a tall step scrolls inside the card while Back and the forward button stay at its foot ([agents](../../artifacts/first-run-setup/agents-820x560-dark.png)); the look step's six themes and both modes fit at 1600 by 1000 in light ([look](../../artifacts/first-run-setup/look-1600x1000-light.png)). The tour lit the sidebar, New thread, the thread area and the Settings link in turn, its note beside a narrow part and inside a wide one, with Skip tour, Back, Next and Done ([sidebar at 820](../../artifacts/first-run-setup/tour-sidebar-820x560-dark.png), [New thread in light](../../artifacts/first-run-setup/tour-new-thread-1600x1000-light.png)).

The design captures now include each setup step and the tour's first stop (`artifacts/design/app-review/baseline/onboarding-step-*.png`, `threads-tour-projects.png`), and every baseline was regenerated with `npm run design:capture` because nearly all of them show the mark; it verified 152 captures, and `npm run design:verify` then matched them. Two earlier full runs failed late, on the Files panel answering "Files is busy." where the phase two capture expects the missing working folder's sentence; that capture passed every time it ran alone or after any one earlier capture group. Files answers at most four requests at once, so the capture now presses its Retry once when it says busy.

## Tests

The onboarding, step, tour, app, mark and widget suites cover the new flow, and `npm test -- --maxWorkers=2` passed except six git working-copy tests in `gitStatus.test.ts`, the thread-worktree suites (now `tests/integration/threadWorktreeAllocation.test.ts`, `tests/integration/threadWorktreeRecovery.test.ts`, `tests/integration/threadWorktreeReclaim.test.ts`, `tests/integration/threadWorktreeSubmodules.test.ts`, `tests/integration/threadWorktreeIdentity.test.ts` and `tests/unit/main/threadWorkingDirectory.test.ts`) and `threadWorktreesNative.test.ts`, which timed out at 15 seconds while Electron builds ran on the same machine. This branch changes nothing they load; rerun on their own with a 90-second timeout they passed, 92 of 92. The Playwright specs that walk setup passed: `app`, `onboarding-microphone-step`, `widget-topmost`, `agentControl`, `agents`, `agentSetup`, `crossing` and `phase-three-theme-branding`. `agentSetup` now starts from a profile past setup, since setup's agents step connects the providers the room used to start without, and `crossing`'s last steps follow the instant New thread that #347 shipped before this branch.
