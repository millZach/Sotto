# Watcher's owl family, October 22, 2026

The night scene moved into the room's side margins with a thread owl for each watched thread (`docs/plans/watcher-owl-family.md`, Zach's picks from `prototype/watcher-owl-family`, issue #1343), checked in Sotto Owl built from the branch, on Windows, with `npx playwright test tests/e2e/watcher-night-scene.spec.ts`. Main's Watcher was stood in for by a seeded projection, as `watcher.spec.ts` does: no Watcher for the first entry, and a Watcher with a short conversation watching three threads of the design fixture (one working, one with a permission waiting, one idle).

## What was checked

- **Only the margins, three sizes, dark and light.** On the capture run's fixed clock every owl sits still. The first entry ([dark, 1280x800](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/first-entry-dark-1280x800.png)), and the conversation with its three thread owls at 1600x1000 ([dark](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/thread-owls-dark-1600x1000.png)), 1280x800 ([light](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/thread-owls-light-1280x800.png)) and the 820x560 minimum, where the margins are 16 pixels and the owls are a rail of eyes ([dark](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/thread-owls-dark-820x560.png), [light](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/thread-owls-light-820x560.png)). The run checks every size in both modes. At each, it reads both canvases between the composer's sides, from the header down, and finds not one painted pixel: 16 readings, every one 0 ([readings](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/readings.json)). Nothing in the room overflows or clips at any size (`pageBoundProblems`); the rail's buttons first stuck 3 pixels past the window's edge there, and are now held inside the pane.
- **An owl for each watched thread.** At every size there is one owl button for each of the three watched threads. The working one has round eyes, the one waiting on a permission glows in the accent, and the idle one's eyes are shut.
- **Watched keeps the scene; Visualize puts it away** (Zach's pick, October 22). On Watched the column reads empty as on Conversation ([dark, 1280x800](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/watched-dark-1280x800.png)). On Visualize both canvases are gone within the fade, and they come back with Conversation.
- **Decoration only.** Both canvases and the owls' button layer are `aria-hidden`; the canvases have no tab stop and take no pointer events.
- **Reduced motion.** With the system's reduced motion Watcher's owl sits in its tree and both thread owls sit in their places from the first frame. Once the scene is still it paints no frame at all over 600 ms ([dark, 1280x800](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/reduced-motion-dark-1280x800.png)). A press in the margin brightens a spot and then fades, and the count holds again.
- **A newcomer, hover and press, on a live clock.** At rest with a glowing owl the scene painted 5 frames a second (it checks 8 times a second and paints only a blink, a star's step or the glow's). When the stand-in published a third watched thread, the moon's whirlpool opened, a ripple spread from it, and the new owl flew down to the limb while Watcher's owl turned to watch ([in flight](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/newcomer-flight-dark-1600x1000.png)). Counted over 1 s from that moment the scene painted 30 frames a second ([readings](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/readings.json)). Hovering an owl shows its thread's name and what it is doing ([the tip](../../artifacts/watcher-owl-family/owl-tip-dark-1600x1000.png)), and pressing it opens the thread beside Watcher. With the composer taken out of the layout no frame is painted or waited for, and painting resumes once it is back.
- The flight's frame count once came back at 1 in a second, with the display's own frames not counted at the time. Three runs after the spec began counting those too all passed, and the message now gives both counts, so a throttled window can be told from a stuck scene.

## Covered by unit tests

`tests/unit/renderer/watcherNightScene.test.ts`: everything is laid out beside the column at three sizes; spots fill the right tree's limb from the column outward, then Watcher's tree, then the ground; a margin under 160 pixels gets the rail, whose every glyph falls in a margin at the smallest window, and a narrower one nothing; nothing is drawn in the column or under words; Watcher's owl sits still at home with motion reduced, flies only home, to the other tree or onto the composer's branch on its own side, passes the moon between the trees, and turns to watch a newcomer or a press; the pace (30 frames a second moving, 8 checks at rest, none still) and its pacing on 60 and 144 hertz displays; stars twinkling in half-second steps; ripples that start later than now; ink from the surface's luminance; a change of pixel ratio. `tests/unit/renderer/watcherOwlFamily.test.ts`: each row's mood; spot assignment, the "+N" pile and a leaving owl holding its spot; owls already watched sitting when the room opens; a newcomer out of the moon's whirlpool, several in turn; an owl going back into the moon and the pile's oldest hopping to its spot; fades with motion reduced and none for a capture; a new mood or title in place; a new size ending every flight; no owl where there is no margin. `tests/unit/renderer/watcherView.test.tsx`: the scene beside the first entry, staying after the first send, staying on Watched, leaving for Visualize and coming back, staying while the conversation reloads, and absent with the Watcher gate off.

## Not covered

- macOS and Linux windows. The scene is drawn the same way on each, and the run is on Windows.
- Themes other than Sotto's default (T3 Code) in the captures. Every colour is read from the theme's tokens and light or dark is decided from the surface, as before.
- Watcher's owl's random flights in the built app. They come 45 to 110 seconds apart; the unit tests check their places and routes, and the flight drawing is the October 21 owl's.
- More threads than spots in the built app. The "+N" pile is checked by the unit tests.
