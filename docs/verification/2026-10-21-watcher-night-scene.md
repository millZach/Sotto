# Watcher's night scene, October 21, 2026

The night scene (`docs/plans/watcher-night-scene.md`, Zach's pick of variant A from `prototype/watcher-owl-scene`, with the owl a quarter smaller) checked in Sotto Owl built from the branch, on Windows, with `npx playwright test tests/e2e/watcher-night-scene.spec.ts`. Main's Watcher was stood in for by a seeded projection, as `watcher.spec.ts` does: no Watcher for the first entry, and a Watcher whose conversation was emptied for the empty conversation.

## What was checked

- **Both empty states, three sizes, dark and light.** On the capture run's fixed clock the scene holds its settled picture, with the owl on the composer's top-right corner. The first entry at 1600x1000 ([dark](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/first-entry-dark-1600x1000.png), [light](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/first-entry-light-1600x1000.png)), 1280x800 ([dark](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/first-entry-dark-1280x800.png), [light](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/first-entry-light-1280x800.png)) and the 820x560 minimum ([dark](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/first-entry-dark-820x560.png), [light](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/first-entry-light-820x560.png)). An empty Watcher conversation at 1280x800 ([dark](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/empty-conversation-dark-1280x800.png), [light](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/empty-conversation-light-1280x800.png)); the run checks it at all three sizes. Nothing in the room overflows or clips at any size (`pageBoundProblems`). Light mode draws the same night in the theme's dark text colour, with more of it, as the prototype does.
- **Text keeps its contrast.** At every size and in both modes, for each line of the intro, the empty conversation's line and the line under the composer, the run reads the scene's canvas under the words' box and blends its strongest pixel there with the surface. The scene draws nothing under words, so the strongest pixel is empty everywhere, and the lowest ratio is the muted line under the composer on its own surface: 9.48:1 dark and 6.72:1 light ([readings](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/readings.json)). Before nothing was drawn under words, the prototype's faint stars under the empty conversation's muted line measured 3.72:1 in light mode at 1600x1000; that is why the plan departs from it there.
- **Decoration only.** Both canvases are `aria-hidden`, have no tab stop and take no pointer events, in every state checked.
- **Reduced motion.** With the system's reduced motion the owl is perched on the composer from the first frame, and the scene draws no frame at all while nothing moves: an end-to-end run counts the frames drawn on Watcher's pane, and the count holds over 600 ms ([dark, 1280x800](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/reduced-motion-dark-1280x800.png)). A press in the background brightens a spot where it landed and nothing travels or bends ([120 ms after the press](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/reduced-motion-press-dark-1280x800.png)); once the spot has faded the count holds again.
- **The flight, a ripple and the fade, on a live clock.** The owl starts in the left tree ([watching](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/flight-1-watching-dark-1280x800.png)), takes off, skims the composer ([flying](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/flight-2-flying-dark-1280x800.png)), and perches on the composer within the flight's twelve seconds. Counted over 1.5 s in flight the scene drew 30 frames a second, and over 2 s perched, 10 ([readings](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/readings.json)). With the composer taken out of the layout the count holds, so no frame is drawn or waited for, and it climbs again once the composer is back. A press in the open background sends out the ring and its echo, which pass behind the intro's words ([450 ms after the press](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/ripple-dark-1280x800.png)). Sending the first message marks both canvases leaving, they are gone within the 0.7 s fade, and the count then holds ([after the send](../../artifacts/watcher-night-scene/after-send-dark-1280x800.png)).
- The same ripple drawn by the prototype at the same moment and size, compared by eye with the capture above: the same ring, echo and bend. The prototype's settled frames at 1600x1000 in both modes were compared the same way with the first entry's captures; the landscape, moon, trees and stars match, and the owl is the quarter smaller Zach asked for.

## Covered by unit tests

`tests/unit/renderer/watcherNightScene.test.ts`: the route ends on the composer's corner at three sizes, and on the top of what stands on the composer; the owl's scale; reduced motion starts settled with no turn or blink; the pace (30, 10 or no frames a second), and frames paced from the last frame's start at about those rates on 60 and 144 hertz displays; a change of pixel ratio laying the scene out again; ripples expire and are capped at eight; light or dark ink from the surface's luminance. `tests/unit/renderer/watcherView.test.tsx`: the scene appears in both empty states, goes with the Watched view and comes back with the conversation, goes while the conversation's history loads or cannot be read and comes back after, fades once the first message is sent and does not come back while it reaches the new Watcher, is absent once a conversation exists, and does not exist with the Watcher gate off.

## Not covered

- macOS and Linux windows. The scene is drawn the same way on each, and the run is on Windows.
- Themes other than Sotto's default (T3 Code) in the captures. The scene reads every colour from the theme's tokens and decides light or dark from the surface, which the unit tests check with colours rather than names.
- The theme changing while the room is open is handled by watching the root's theme attributes; it was not captured.
- A display whose scale changes while the room is open (moving the window to another monitor) lays the scene out again through a resolution query that the unit tests check; it was not tried on a second monitor.
