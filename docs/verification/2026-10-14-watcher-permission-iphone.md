# Watcher's permissions, own changes and watching on the iPhone

Branch `feat/watcher-permission-iphone`, built on Sol's runtime commit `25a1d686d` (the contract in `w2-contract.md`, the host's `watcher-phone-manage` and SottoCore's models for it). Windows 11 on LAPTOP-RUSSH2J5, October 14, 2026. The design is Zach's picks of October 13 from the approved mock-up (`zstack-scratch/watcher-permission/index.html`): control A, mark B and watch B, on the iPhone too.

**Native iPhone app: NOT VERIFIED.** No Swift was compiled, run or tested on this machine: there is no Xcode or Swift toolchain here. The Native iOS job compiles it, runs the SottoAppModel tests and the Focus journeys on the SE and the Pro Max (accessibility-large text, reduced motion). Until that job is green on both phones and the screenshots named below have been opened and read, none of this is proven on a phone.

## What it does

- **Watcher's permissions (control A).** A chip over the Ask Watcher box names what Watcher may do without asking, in the words the iPhone's New thread already uses: Ask before actions, Allow edits, Allow actions, Full access. It opens a sheet with the modes Watcher's model offers, each with a line saying what Watcher does under it, and the mock-up's foot. A pick shows at once and goes as `watcher-phone-set-permission`. Until the provider confirms it, the chip's edge is dashed and a caption says what is still in force ("Codex stays on Ask before actions until it confirms."). Without Can answer on that computer the sheet says the iPhone can only keep Watcher asking first, and the other modes can't be chosen; the computer checks again and its refusal is said in its words.
- **Its own changes (mark B).** Under a reply in which Watcher changed files itself, after the reply's What Watcher did card, one quiet line: "Watcher changed 2 files itself." and its reason, or "It gave no reason." in the needs-you ink. "Show the 2 files" lists them. It reads the page's `ownChanges`; a mark whose reply isn't shown is left out.
- **Watching (watch B).** A thread's page has Watch beside its state, on while Watcher watches it. Watched says what watching means, has Watch a thread (a sheet of the threads this iPhone can reach that Watcher doesn't watch) and Stop all, and each card says what Watcher last did and has Stop watching. A press acts at once and says so with Undo for eight seconds. A watched thread's reply box says Watcher keeps it going, or waits while the user's own turn runs. There is no watch limit and no "3 of 4 watched".
- **The wire.** The hello opts in to `watcher-phone-manage`. Each change goes under its own request ID. After a lost acknowledgement the iPhone reads that ID's `watcherManage` receipt, then Watcher's page, and says what happened or that it isn't confirmed; nothing is sent again on its own.
- **Older computers.** A computer that doesn't offer `watcher-phone-manage` shows no chip, no Watch and no Stop watching, keeps the old meter and room line on Watched, and keeps "Watched by Watcher. If you send here, Watcher stops sending to it." in the reply box, which is still true there.

It found no compile error and no failing assertion. On its advice the ternaries in the fixture's large literals and the own-change line's run of words became typed constants, so the type checker can't time out on them. Reading is not compiling.
- **The job's time.** On a push to `main` ([run 38512275242](https://github.com/millZach/Sotto/actions/runs/38512275242)) the Native iOS job took 106 of its 110 minutes, from journeys that took 20 to 312 seconds each on each phone. ## Tests

## Judgement calls

- **Labels.** The iPhone's own names for the four modes, as the approved phone frames use, rather than the desktop's Ask for approval and Auto. Full access's line names the computer instead of "this computer", which on a phone would mean the phone.
- **The chip goes while typing.** It is put away while the box has the keyboard, so the SE keeps room for the conversation above it.
- **Where Watch goes.** To the computer that watches the thread; otherwise to the thread's own computer when it runs Watcher, then to the Watcher tab's. Watcher's own thread is never offered. Watch a thread lists only threads on computers reached now, and not settled ones.
- **Undo and VoiceOver.** The toast never takes focus; it is announced, and Watch or Stop watching where the press was made is the way back once it has gone.
- **No longer watched** stays on Watched as before. With standing watches, a thread Watcher only took part in through a request, without a watch, reads there too, as the projection says.
- **Not changed here.** `CONTEXT.md` (the desktop branch coins Own-change line; Standing watch and Watched thread are already in), and ADR-0073's original line that a watched thread's composer says a send stops Watcher, which the October 13 amendment overtakes. SottoCore is unchanged: its models were complete.
