# Phase 3 final visual fixes: verification

These are fixes for the four findings in `artifacts/phase-three-final-review/visual-review-result.md`. The target is the Windows Electron app at 1600, 1280 and 820×560, in dark and light. It was verified in the built app (`out/main/index.js` from this worktree) with owned `sotto-e2e-*` profiles and fixture providers. The spec is `tests/e2e/phase-three-final-visual-fixes.spec.ts` and the captures are in `artifacts/phase-three-final-visual-fixes/`.

| # | Finding | Change | Rendered check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Custom and imported names cut off: invisible actions took 112px | `.theme-card__footer` wraps (`wrap-reverse`). A name that does not fit beside its actions gets the whole width, up to two lines, then a clamp. The actions sit above it and still show on hover or focus. The card is a column with the footer at its bottom edge. The title tooltip is the name. | At 1600, 1280 and 820, every name, including "Morning Harbor", "Catppuccin Macchiato", "Catppuccin Community" and a 48-character name, has no clipping, at most 2 lines, and no overlap with the actions. The accessible name contains the full name. From the keyboard: title → Duplicate → Edit → Export → Remove, with the actions at opacity 1, Enter on Edit opens "Edit theme", and a pointer click on the title uses the theme. `gallery-custom-{1600,1280,820}-dark`, `gallery-custom-hover-{1280,820}-light`, `card-long-name-keyboard-remove-1600-dark` |
| 2 | Preview circles overlapped by 12px, so rings and badges collided | T3 geometry: 68px buttons 10px apart with 56px orbs, and the badge inside its own button (no negative margin or z-index stacking) | For every two-circle card at all three sizes, the gap between buttons is 10px and each ring and badge lies inside its own circle, clear of the other. `card-tide-both-halves-1600-dark`, `card-harbor-review-both-halves-1600-dark` |

## Limits

- At 820 the Tools panel overlays the thread composer, which is existing Tools behaviour and not the editor bar. Send is under the panel there, and the spec only asserts that the bar never covers Send.
- A theme name that runs past two lines is clamped. The whole name stays in the tooltip and the accessible name.
- Not rerun: display scaling other than the machine's 150%, and macOS.
- Since #304, the spec saves a draft in the gated pane before it opens. Stop now takes Send's place in a working thread's empty composer, and the prompt stays off while a permission is pending, so a draft is the only way to show the disabled Send whose title gives the instruction. A rerun therefore captures the draft where the placeholder was, and shows the instruction only in Queue prompt's tooltip. The spec no longer covers the empty composer with Stop alone. The committed `permission-*` and `form-and-permission-grid-*` images predate the change and are kept as the record of that empty composer.
