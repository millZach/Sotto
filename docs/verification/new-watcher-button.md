# New Watcher, October 21, 2026

New Watcher in Watcher's composer (Zach's pick of variant A from `prototype/new-watcher-button`; ADR-0073's amendment of the same date) checked in Sotto Owl built from the branch, on Windows, with `npm run build` and then `npx playwright test tests/e2e/watcher.spec.ts`. Two journeys cover it. The first stands in for main's Watcher with a seeded projection, as the rest of `watcher.spec.ts` does, so the room can be held busy and captured on a fixed picture; Streaming WAV stall, emptied, stands for the new Watcher because it is on the same model as the seeded Watcher. The second runs against main's own Watcher over the fake Codex app server, with nothing seeded.

## What was checked

- **Where it stands.** In Watcher's composer the chip reads **New Watcher** with the pen icon, first in the footer row where an ordinary thread's composer says where it runs, beside the model and permission chips and styled as one of them ([dark, 1280x800](../../artifacts/new-watcher-button/idle-dark-1280x800.png), [dark, 820x560](../../artifacts/new-watcher-button/idle-dark-820x560.png), [light, 1280x800](../../artifacts/new-watcher-button/idle-light-1280x800.png)). Watcher's composer shows no host chip. Its accessible name is its visible words, New Watcher, and its title is **Start a new Watcher with a handover of this one**.
- **Keyboard.** Tab from the message box reaches New Watcher, then the model chip. The paperclip comes before it in the row; on the seeded model, which takes no images, the paperclip is off and out of the Tab order, so Tab goes from the box straight to New Watcher. Enter on New Watcher presses it.
- **While Watcher works.** With Watcher's turn running, the chip is dimmed, stays in the Tab order, and says **Wait for Watcher to finish before starting a new one.** in its title and to a screen reader through its description. Enter on it does nothing ([dark, 1280x800](../../artifacts/new-watcher-button/busy-dark-1280x800.png)).
- **After a press.** The new Watcher starts at once. The handover strip stands over its composer and says **Sending starts the new Watcher with a handover of the last one.**, naming no provider, with **It carries all 2 messages.** and **Read it**; the send button reads **Continue**; focus is in the new composer. The empty conversation says **New Watcher on GPT-5.4.** and **The last conversation is in Watcher history on the Threads page. Watcher still watches your 2 threads, and the threads it started keep working.**, where a Watcher with no earlier conversation says **What is next for this thread?** The chip now says **This Watcher is new, so there is nothing to hand over yet.** ([dark, 1280x800](../../artifacts/new-watcher-button/after-dark-1280x800.png), [dark, 820x560](../../artifacts/new-watcher-button/after-dark-820x560.png), [light, 1280x800](../../artifacts/new-watcher-button/after-light-1280x800.png)). Escape in the composer puts the offer away and the send button returns.
- **Against main's own Watcher.** After a first exchange with the fake Codex, a press makes main start a new Watcher on the same provider, model and permission mode, move the last conversation to Watcher history, and offer its handover from the same provider; the strip names no provider, the composer has focus, and the empty conversation's note shows.
- **Sizes and contrast.** At 1600x1000, 1280x800 and the 820x560 minimum, dark and light, before and after the press: nothing in the room overflows or clips (`pageBoundProblems`), the floating switch covers no composer, the chip is wholly in view, and every piece of text the room measures holds 4.5:1 or more on its own surface ([readings](../../artifacts/new-watcher-button/readings.json)). The unavailable chip is dimmed on purpose, as the prototype draws it and as an inactive control may be: measured from the captures, its words are 3.25:1 in dark and 2.41:1 in light, against 9.20:1 and 6.95:1 when it is available. The room's own measure reads the chip's colour before the dimming, so its readings overstate the unavailable chip; these two figures are from the pixels.
- **Stable.** With a projection seeded, stable shows no New Watcher, as it shows nothing else of Watcher.

## Covered by tests

`tests/integration/watcherStartNew.test.ts`, over the fake providers: New Watcher starts a Watcher on the same provider, model, effort and permission mode, moves the last one to Watcher history, revokes its tools, keeps standing watches, receipts and a worker's watch, and offers the handover; it refuses with no Watcher, for a Watcher that has said nothing, while a start is still working, while a delivery's result is unknown, while Watcher's turn runs, when the model is not ready, and when the thread reports a permission mode other than the saved one, each time changing nothing; a New Watcher stopped part-way is finished by the next press, or by opening with its saved choice, and offers the same provider's handover, while a model change after it, and a first Watcher finished on a second try, offer none. `tests/unit/main/watcherRoomIpc.test.ts`, `tests/unit/main/watcherRoomDesktop.test.ts` and `tests/unit/preload/watcherRoom.test.ts`: the channel exists only with Watcher on, takes no input, and is answered and flushed like the room's other calls. `tests/unit/renderer/watcherNew.test.tsx`: the chip's place and names, its two reasons, a press and a refusal in main's words, the strip's lead with and without a provider change, the empty conversation's note and its sentence in every combination. `tests/unit/renderer/watcherHandover.test.tsx`: Escape from the composer puts the offer away even when the editor's own keymap took the key, unless a composer menu is open.

## Not covered

- macOS and Linux windows. The chip is one of the composer's own chips there too, and the run is on Windows.
- Reduced motion was not captured for this change: the chip and the note add no motion, and the handover strip is the one already checked under ADR-0073.
- The iPhone and Android clients, which have no New Watcher; host protocol version 1 is unchanged.
