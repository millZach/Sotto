On October 13, 2026 Zach picked from the [permission and watching mock-up](C:/Users/zache/AppData/Local/zstack-scratch/watcher-permission/index.html), which stays outside the repository: control A (Watcher's composer gets the Threads composer's permission chip), mark B (one quiet line under a reply in which Watcher changed files itself) and watch B (a Watch toggle in a thread's pane header, an owl toggle on each thread row, and a Watched view in the room like the phone's Watched tab). ## What I worked out

- **The modes' lines are the mock-up's.** Sotto had no one-line descriptions for its four modes, so the chip uses the approved mock-up's, with Auto's naming Watcher's provider. Threads keeps its list as it was.
- **What is in force.** The chip shows the choice main remembers (`runtimeMode`), and measures it against the current Watcher's mode (`currentOptions.runtimeMode`, or the thread's own where the projection has none). Before Watcher exists nothing is in force, so nothing is dashed. Where the bridge has no `setPermission` (main before Sol's runtime), the mode is said as a fact, in Sotto's words.
- **Where the tabs go, and the count.** The mock-up puts Conversation and Watched right after All threads and shows no in-flight meter, so the meter is gone and the limit stays only in More Watcher actions. Beside a thread at 820 by 560, Watcher's name steps aside for the tabs (the tile says whose room it is; the heading stays for screen readers) rather than truncating to "Watc…".
- **The toast's place.** The mock-up draws it above the composer of the pane in view. It sits at the top of the focused pane's composer area, so it never covers a composer at any size; a press with no pane open shows no toast, and the toggle's pressed state still says it.
- **The sidebar row's owl is among its actions.** Zach's pick puts an owl toggle on each row; the sidebar already shows a row's controls on hover, focus and selection, so the owl is one of them, and the always-visible owl after the logo stays the mark. In All threads, a light list with room to spare, the owl toggle is always shown, as drawn.
- **Watched without the window's row.** A watched thread on a host this window does not list keeps its card, with the room's own title and status, so it can still be stopped.
- **What Watcher last did** comes from Sotto's records only: its latest action on the thread and how the delivery went, how long a standing watch has run with nothing sent, or that the user's own turn holds it. The mock-up's prose lines ("Watcher asked it to rerun the checks") would need words main does not project.
- **Show change per file** is not built: the mock-up drew a link to each file's diff, which needs a diff the projection does not carry. The brief asks for the file names on request.
- **Watcher history threads** offer no Watch, as Watcher itself does not.

- Watch from a pane header from the keyboard, the toast and Undo, Stop watching under the composer, the sidebar row's owl reached by Tab from the row through Rename and Regenerate title, the Watched tab by the arrow keys, Stop watching on a card, Watch a thread into All threads, and the owl as a row's last stop there, pressed with Space.
- The default theme in dark and light, at 1600 by 1000, 1280 by 800 and the 820 by 560 minimum, with no overflow or clipping, and every text the room draws at 4.5:1 or more on the surface under it, list, caption, line, toast, tabs and cards included (`readings.json`). The lit toggles' fill is the accent mixed into the surface, so the reading is of what shows.
- Reduced motion: the list and the toast open without rising.
- Stable shows no chip, owl toggle, toast or watched note.
