# Transcribe with OpenRouter, October 29, 2026

Settings → Dictation's Speech model group gained **Transcribe with OpenRouter** and OpenRouter's ranked speech models, as the Owl feature `cloud-dictation` ([ADR-0160](../adr/0160-dictation-can-be-transcribed-by-an-openrouter-speech-model.md)). Checked on forge (Omarchy, Linux) in the built app on a nested desktop, in unit tests, and against OpenRouter's live public lists.

## What was checked

- **The group in the built app.** `tests/e2e/cloud-dictation.spec.ts`, run with `node scripts/verify-linux-window-minimum.mjs --exec … playwright test`, feature on: the switch leads the group, off; turned on it saves `cloudTranscription`, the line saying the saved key is used shows, and the model picker gives way to the ranked list with its top five, Qwen3-ASR 1.7B first and MAI-Transcribe-2 chosen as the default. A click saves `cloudSpeechModel`, Arrow Down from a focused row saves the next and moves focus to it, **Show all 7 models** shows the rest, and the choice is there after a reload. With the feature off neither the switch nor the list shows. Both tests passed. The launcher's closing complaint about `~/.sotto` is the live host's own writes, not the run.
- **Screenshots** with a row's tooltip showing: dark and light at 1280x800 (`artifacts/cloud-dictation/cloud-dictation-1280x800-dark.png`, `artifacts/cloud-dictation/cloud-dictation-1280x800-light.png`) and dark at the 820x560 minimum (`artifacts/cloud-dictation/cloud-dictation-820x560-dark.png`). At the minimum the speed column goes and the tooltip says the time instead; nothing clips. The rows show the measured error rates and times.
- **Two faults the run found, fixed.** A focused row's tooltip lay over **Show all** and took its click, so the tooltip now lets the pointer through. The saved-key line used the disclosure style, which stopped its divider short of the card's edge; it is a full row now.
- **The app's own request, live.** `CloudTranscriptionService` and `OpenRouterSpeechModels`, bundled as they ship and run against OpenRouter with Zach's key (the Cleanup key from his laptop's Sotto): MAI-Transcribe-2 got the dictionary as its Azure phrase list beside zero-retention routing and came back word for word; Voxtral Mini Transcribe 2 went without zero-retention routing, as its refused route requires, and came back word for word in 0.9 s. Qwen3-ASR 1.7B, first in the ranking, timed out at 15 s, and ten more tries at it all hung for 20 s while its host's other models answered in about a second; that is why Zach chose MAI as the default.
- **The ranking.** All 29 models measured ([perf note](../perf/2026-10-29-openrouter-speech-models.md)), $1.10.
- **OpenRouter's lists, live.** `GET /api/v1/models?output_modalities=transcription` answered 29 speech models and `GET /api/v1/endpoints/zdr` named a zero-retention host for 22 of them, both without a key. The parser and the ranking were written against those answers.

## Covered by unit tests

`tests/unit/main/cloudTranscriptionService.test.ts`: the request body (model, base64 WAV, zero-retention routing, bounded spelling hints), the default model when none is chosen, no key, a model that refuses hints asked again without them and not offered them again, every HTTP answer's reason, one retry for a server error or a dropped connection, the rate-limit waits and their diagnostics with nothing said or secret in them, cancel and a reply without text. `tests/unit/main/openRouterSpeechModels.test.ts`: both lists read without a key, the ranking, prices only where plainly per second, the ten-minute reuse and the last good list kept. `tests/unit/renderer/features/dictation/cloudDictation.test.ts`: each OpenRouter reason kept with its own words, no recording without a key, the widget told the audio went out and history told `openrouter`, and Transcribe on this computer sending only the parts OpenRouter turned away, by the local route. `tests/unit/renderer/features/settings/settingsCloudTranscription.test.tsx`, `tests/unit/renderer/dictateRoomCloud.test.tsx` and `tests/unit/renderer/widget/widgetDictation.test.tsx`: the group on and off, the key field, keyboard picks, the list's failure and a model OpenRouter dropped; the Dictate room's buttons and their keyboard order; the widget's tooltip.
