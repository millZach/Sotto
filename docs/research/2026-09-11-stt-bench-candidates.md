# Sotto: OpenRouter transcription bench candidates

Checked 2026-09-11. Research only: no inference calls or benchmark runs.

**Bench Parakeet v3, Voxtral Mini Transcribe 2, GPT Transcribe, Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite alongside MAI-Transcribe-2. Keep Moonshine as the reference.**

Today’s shipped presets are Moonshine-base ONNX q8 (“instant”) and whisper-tiny (“fast”), running through transformers.js in an Electron worker. Parakeet is planned, not shipped. July Moonshine results: **185/465 ms for 2.0/4.4 seconds; 6.0% mean normalized WER; 19.2% proper-noun WER**. [Catalog](../../src/main/models/modelManager.ts), [runtime](../../src/renderer/src/transcription/runtime.ts), [July benchmark](../perf/2026-07-28-asr-model-benchmark.md).

The default [Models API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models) returns 443 entries without dedicated STT. **`GET /api/v1/models?output_modalities=transcription` returns 21**, with architecture `audio->transcription`; the [STT collection](https://openrouter.ai/collections/speech-to-text-models) is another discovery surface. All 21 have listed providers in `/api/v1/models/{id}/endpoints`. [Live catalog](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models?output_modalities=transcription).

All 21 use **T = POST `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/audio/transcriptions`**. Bearer authentication; multipart `file,model` or JSON `model,input_audio:{data,format}`; parse `text` and `usage.cost`. MAI’s OpenRouter upstream is Azure; direct Azure remains out of scope. [Route](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/multimodal/stt), [MAI endpoint](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/microsoft/mai-transcribe-2/endpoints).

Recommended five additions (in priority order):

1. **[Parakeet v3](https://openrouter.ai/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3):** tests the planned standard through OpenRouter and merits a separate local run; its published 81.48% three-day availability makes failures essential to report.
2. **[Voxtral Mini Transcribe 2](https://openrouter.ai/mistralai/voxtral-mini-transcribe):** .35s aggregate latency plus [native `context_bias`](https://docs.mistral.ai/studio/audio/speech_to_text/offline_transcription) for 100 phrases; verify forwarding.
3. **[GPT Transcribe](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/speech-to-text):** the August successor improves on 4o/mini’s [AA accuracy](https://artificialanalysis.ai/speech-to-text/non-streaming) and offers native `prompt,keywords,languages`; verify forwarding.
4. **[google/gemini-3.8-flash](https://openrouter.ai/google/gemini-3.8-flash):** re-screen the newer Flash family as a vocabulary-prompted chat control.
5. **[google/gemini-3.5-flash-lite](https://openrouter.ai/google/gemini-3.5-flash-lite):** the second chat control tests a cheaper, latency-oriented option.

Chat controls use **C = POST `/api/v1/chat/completions` with `input_audio`** and text hints; [SSE streams output](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api/reference/streaming), not live microphone input. [Audio contract](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/multimodal/audio).

Chat estimates assume [32 audio tokens/second](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/audio), 100 prompt tokens/request, 200 output tokens/audio-minute and no reasoning, at standard rates; actual tokens decide billing.

Skip gpt-audio-mini and chat Voxtral initially: July recorded timeout rejection/provider errors, without evidence of a new latency advantage; July’s fastest chat TTFT was 752 ms. [July findings](../perf/2026-07-28-asr-model-benchmark.md). Skip older MAI/OpenAI models, expensive Chirp and slower/heavier alternatives; Nova3’s keyterms are absent from OpenRouter’s documented passthrough allowlist. Qwen0.6B remains a useful local reserve. [Parakeet v2](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2) also merits local testing (CC-BY-4.0; OA 4.70%). Flux STT, AssemblyAI Universal, Scribe, Canary and newer Qwen-Audio-3.0 ASR are absent from the checked catalog; vendor availability is insufficient. Scribe v2’s 2.2% AA WER does not supply an OpenRouter route. [AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/speech-to-text/non-streaming).

Privacy: audio and hints leave the machine. OpenRouter permits audio-retention exceptions for abuse, security, billing and law. [Policy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy/). Its [ZDR inventory](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/endpoints/zdr) includes MAI/Azure, Parakeet/Together and Gemini/Vertex. [Together](https://docs.together.ai/docs/privacy-and-security) permits temporary performance caching. [Mistral’s default endpoint](https://openrouter.ai/mistralai/voxtral-mini-transcribe) retains 30 days; only its EU endpoint is flagged ZDR. [OpenRouter flags GPT retention](https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-transcribe), while [OpenAI’s transcription-specific policy](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) says none: unresolved discrepancy. Gemini AI Studio retains abuse-monitoring data 55 days; prefer Vertex for chat. [Google policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/usage-policies).

Corrections to the [MAI brief](2026-09-10-mai-transcribe-2.md): retain its multipart/JSON route and `provider.options.azure.phraseList.phrases`; update [aggregate p50](https://openrouter.ai/microsoft/mai-transcribe-2) from ~.5s to .35s; OpenRouter quotes **$0.10/hour = $0.00002778/second**. Top-level multipart `prompt` is ignored; pass hints under `provider.options.<endpoint tag>`, with integration-specific forwarding. [STT guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/multimodal/stt). **STT ignores provider pinning and per-request data-policy controls**, so do not promise a selected Whisper host, Mistral EU route or enforced ZDR. [Routing limitations](https://openrouter.ai/blog/tutorials/transcription-on-openrouter/). Replace Azure-first/Parakeet-baseline recommendations with this OpenRouter-only screen against current Moonshine. Measure full parsed-transcript p50/p95, failures and exact coding/name spelling with identical hints and without hints; aggregate WER cannot establish identifier fidelity.
