Choosing a level, with the provider taking its time

Three copies of the same card. Drag the thumb to Max, or press End, and watch the word above it. In now the word previews under your finger, snaps back to the old level the instant you let go, then blinks to the new one when the provider answers. The trace under each card is what the user can see happening. Try it at 150ms and at 3s, and then on Fails, which is the case each variant has to survive.

What to watch for when it reaches the top

The arrival is the part that reads as a blink. Set the provider to 3s, then press End: in now nothing at all happens for three seconds and then the word, the tide and the composer outline all fire together. In A they fire under your hand. In B the word goes at once but the tide and the outline hold until it is real, so the card says Max over a composer that is still dark.
