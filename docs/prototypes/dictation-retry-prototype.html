PROTOTYPE · THROWAWAY

Dictation retry in the widget

A long dictation goes up in parts. Today, when OpenRouter turns one part away (HTTP 429), Sotto throws the whole dictation away and shows the error for 500 ms.

What the fix does in every variant: Sotto first waits and retries the part by itself (about 1, 2 and 4 s). Only if that still fails does the widget ask you. The audio stays in memory, never on disk, until you try again, discard it, or start a new dictation.

The widget never takes focus, so keys are global while the error shows. Esc discards. Your dictation hotkey starts a new dictation, which also discards. Try again is a click in A, B and C.
