Help

Sotto is small on purpose. This page covers everything it does.

Press the shortcutCtrlShiftSpace works in any application. The widget opens and starts listening.
Speak naturallyPause when you like. Press the shortcut again when you're finished, or Escape to cancel.
Your words appearThe transcript is copied, then pasted at your cursor. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What if another app already uses my shortcut?

Sotto keeps your previous working shortcut active. Choose a different combination in Settings and apply it again.

Recording won't start.

Open Windows Settings, then Privacy & security, then Microphone, and allow desktop apps. Then choose an available input in Sotto's Settings.

What leaves this computer?

Speech and transcripts stay here. Sotto has no account, analytics, cloud transcription, or per-use fee.

Two optional features do use the network: installing the Multi-lingual model contacts Hugging Face, and AI formatting sends transcript text to OpenRouter. Both are off until you turn them on, and audio is never sent.

Which model should I use?

Standard is included, works offline, and is English-only. Multi-lingual is an optional download for non-English speech. Installing it sends ordinary network metadata, such as your IP address, to Hugging Face.

The text was copied but not pasted.

Sotto is clipboard first: successful text is always copied. Automatic paste may be blocked in elevated, protected, or password fields and in applications with custom input handling. Paste manually with CtrlV.

What does resetting do?

Resetting settings reopens first-run setup. It does not remove models or clear transcript history. Use the History controls when you want to remove saved text.
