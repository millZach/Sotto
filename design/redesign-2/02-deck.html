Getting started
How do I dictate?

Press Ctrl Shift Space in any application to begin, then press it again to finish. Press Esc to cancel an active recording without transcribing. The transcript is copied, then pasted at your cursor.

Another app already uses my shortcut

Sotto keeps your previous working shortcut active. Choose a different combination in Settings and apply it again.

Which model should I use?

Standard is included, works offline, and is English-only. Multi-lingual is an optional download for non-English speech.

Privacy
What leaves this computer?

Speech and transcripts stay here. Sotto has no account, analytics, cloud transcription, or per-use fee.

Installing the Multi-lingual model contacts Hugging Face, and AI formatting sends transcript text to OpenRouter. Both are off until you turn them on. Audio is never sent.

Troubleshooting
Recording won't start

Open Windows Settings, then Privacy & security, then Microphone, and allow desktop apps. Then choose an available input in Sotto's Settings.

Text was copied but not pasted

Automatic paste may be blocked in elevated, protected, or password fields and in applications with custom input handling. Paste manually with Ctrl V.

What does resetting do?

Resetting settings reopens first-run setup. It does not remove models or clear transcript history.
