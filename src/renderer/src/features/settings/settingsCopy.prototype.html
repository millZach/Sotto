Settings wording, before and after

Each row is one piece of text on a settings page. Before is what Sotto says today and After is my rewrite. Where a detail was dropped, the line under the rewrite says where it moves, usually docs/guide.md. Mark a row Looks good or Change, add a note if you like, then press Copy my notes and paste the result back to me. Rows I left alone are listed at the end of each page. Notes are not saved, so copy them before you close this page.

Not in this pass: error and status messages (they follow the error rule: what happened, what was lost, what to do), and dialogs that open from Settings. Blue italic marks a name filled in when Sotto runs.
